# MetallurgyPlus

<p>
MetallurgyPlus is a technic Minecraft mod which focuses on more ores, materials, alloys as well as [machines](#machines).
Here is a list of all added [resources](resources.md).
This mod is by far not realistic, but it is rooted in reality. 
Because of this recipes and (especially) ore generation have been stylized to preserve the fun.
</p>

[CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/metallurgyplus)

<br>

## Machines
Machines are added to give the player a built-in solution to gather/produce/process/craft other resources.

### Alloy Smelter ![Alloy Smelter](src/main/resources/assets/metallurgyplus/textures/block/alloy_smelter_front.png)
Processes multiple metals and materials to produce alloys.

### Ore Processing Unit ![Ore Processing Unit](src/main/resources/assets/metallurgyplus/textures/block/ore_processing_unit_front.png)
Processes raw pieces of ores to produce raw pieces of resources.

### Grinder ![Grinder](src/main/resources/assets/metallurgyplus/textures/block/grinder_front.png)
Grinds ingots and raw pieces into their respective dust variant.

### Metal Press ![Metal Press](src/main/resources/assets/metallurgyplus/textures/block/press_front.png)
Presses ingots into their respective plate variant.

### Quarry ![Quarry](src/main/resources/assets/metallurgyplus/textures/block/quarry_front.png)
Excavates a user-specified area and produces their block drops.

### Cables
Transfers energy across multiple blocks.

### Batteries
Stores vast amounts of energy.

### Solar Panels ![Solar Panel](src/main/resources/assets/metallurgyplus/textures/block/solar_panel_top.png)
Produces vast amounts of <span style='color: green;'>eco-friendly</span> energy.
